Questions

Buying from SuPlushies

Something else? Send us a message.

Are your products licensed?+

Yes. Everything we carry is officially licensed. Each license sets the countries where a product can be sold, so we confirm which items are available for your market before you order.

Who can buy from SuPlushies?+

We sell wholesale to retailers: brick-and-mortar stores, chains and online sellers. We don't sell to individual shoppers, but they can find a store that carries us on our store finder.

How do I get wholesale pricing?+

Apply for a wholesale account. Once you're approved, usually within 1–2 business days, we send our price list with case packs and current availability.

What kinds of products do you carry?+

Blind boxes, character plush from 4 to 20 inches, plush and vinyl keychains, display figures, trading cards and stickers, from Pokémon, Sanrio, Disney, popular anime and designer-toy brands.

Where do you ship from?+

SuPlushies has offices in the US and China and distributes worldwide. Tell us where your store is and where you sell, and we'll quote the items licensed for your market and the best way to ship them.

Can you supply a whole toy section for a chain?+

Yes. We work with multi-location retailers as the single supplier for their toy sections, choosing the assortment and refreshing it as trends change.

Can SuPlushies be the only toy vendor for our supermarket's toy aisles?+

Yes. Supermarkets and grocery stores use us as their single toy supplier. We choose an assortment for your shoppers and shelf space, ship it by the display so it's quick to put out, and refresh it as trends change. When you apply, tell us how many aisles or feet of shelf you have for toys.

Don't see your kind of store?

If you have customers and shelf space, we can probably help. Tell us about your store.
