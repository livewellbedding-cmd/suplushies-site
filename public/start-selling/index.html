FAQ

Questions new sellers ask

Can I buy wholesale if I'm just starting out?

Yes. SuPlushies works with first-time store owners, people adding a toy shelf to an existing shop, and online sellers, as well as established chains. Tell us your budget and timeline in the quote form and we'll suggest a starter assortment that fits.

Do I need a physical store?

No. Online stores and marketplace sellers are welcome. Tell us where you sell and which countries you ship to, and we'll quote the items licensed for those markets.

Which countries can you supply?

SuPlushies has offices in the US and China and distributes worldwide. Character licenses are set country by country, so we confirm which items you can sell in your market before you order.

Are the toys officially licensed?

Yes. Everything in the catalog is officially licensed, from Pokémon and Sanrio to Disney, Chiikawa and popular anime series.

What do I need to open a wholesale account?

To get a quote, just your business details. Before your first order we'll ask for your tax ID (EIN in the US, or your VAT or business registration number elsewhere) and a resale certificate or local equivalent.

Is there a minimum order?

Tell us your budget when you request a quote. We'll build a starter assortment around it and explain the order options for your account.

Can I get samples?

Ask for samples in your quote request and we'll let you know what's available for the items you're interested in.

What sells best for a new toy shop?

Blind boxes and keychains are easy impulse buys at the counter, plush draws people in, and figures and trading cards bring collectors back. Pokémon, Sanrio, Disney Stitch and Chiikawa are reliable starting points.

How fast will I hear back?

We reply to quote requests within 1–2 business days.

Tell us about your plan. We reply within 1–2 business days.
