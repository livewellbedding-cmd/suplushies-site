Workhorse

“Keeps the wheels turning.”

Workhorse is the family's quiet backbone, first one up and last one to rest. He believes love looks like showing up, every single day. The Mighty Zoo is teaching him that leaning on others isn't weakness; it's how a family stays strong.

Superpower
Showing up, every single day
Growing edge
Letting others share the load
Best match
Chill Panda
Series
Series 1: The Mighty Zoo

Personality profile

The one who keeps the Mighty Zoo running

On a good day
Up before the sun, fixing the water pump, hauling breakfast and humming while he does it. Everything works because he made it work.
On a bad day
Runs on empty, skips meals and snaps at whoever is closest. Then feels terrible about it for the rest of the day.
What’s really going on underneath
Believes love looks like showing up. He’s scared that if he stops, things will fall apart and it will be his fault.
Favorite spot in the Mighty Zoo
The feed barn, first thing in the morning.
Never without
A tool belt that is never missing a tool, a red work vest and a backpack full of just-in-case supplies.
Says things like
“I’ve got it.”
“Already fixed. Don’t worry about it.”
Learning to
Letting others share the load, and saying sorry when tired turns into cranky.
Closest friends
Chill Panda, who reminds him to sit down, and Generous Piggy, who makes sure he eats.
Sparks fly with
Fearless Cub, whose eager help sometimes means more work. They’re learning to work as a team.

Nobody in the Mighty Zoo is all good or all bad. A bad day is part of the story, never the whole story.

You know a Workhorse if…

  • They're the first one up and the last to rest.
  • They answer “Do you need help?” with “I've got it.”
  • They fixed the thing before you noticed it was broken.

How to love him

  • Take one task off their list for good, not just once.
  • Notice the invisible work out loud.
  • Protect their rest the way they protect everyone else's.

Stories and life lessons with Workhorse

Workhorse is the main character in 1 story and shows up in 2 more.

Home and family

Fearless Cub and the Other Side of the Fence

Fearless Cub slips out of the zoo to see the big world, and learns what home really is when the storm rolls in.

The lessonBe curious, and go explore. But the grass isn’t always greener on the other side. Home isn’t only where your heart is; it’s your shelter from the storm. When the sky turns dark, bet on family.

3 min read
Asking for help

Workhorse’s Grumpy Morning

Workhorse is up before the sun and too tired to be kind. A bad morning, it turns out, is not the same as a bad heart.

The lessonA bad morning doesn’t make a bad heart. When you snap, say sorry. Then rest, eat something, and let someone carry a bucket.

2 min read
Big feelings

Chill Panda Loses His Cool

Everyone brings their problems to calm, steady Panda. Nobody asks how Panda is doing, until the day he shouts.

The lessonCalm friends have storms too. Ask the steady ones how they’re doing, and wait for the real answer.
