Chaos Cat

“Rules? Never heard of 'em.”

Chaos is the spark, the dare, the surprise. The Zoo is helping him channel that wild energy into joy, and the occasional, mostly harmless prank.

Superpower
Joy, surprise and play
Growing edge
Making sure the fun is fun for everyone
Best match
Negative Natalie
Series
Series 1: The Mighty Zoo

Personality profile

The spark, the dare and the surprise

On a good day
Turns a boring afternoon into an adventure, gets everyone laughing and dares friends to try the thing they were scared of.
On a bad day
Takes a joke too far, knocks something over, or, when everyone assumes the worst, decides to live up to it.
What’s really going on underneath
Wants to be included and trusted. A bad reputation stings more than he lets on.
Favorite spot in the Mighty Zoo
Anywhere he isn’t supposed to be, usually the top of the fence post.
Never without
A red ball of yarn that goes everywhere he goes, and leaves a trail.
Says things like
“Rules? Never heard of ’em.”
“Okay, but what if we did it anyway?”
Learning to
Making sure the fun is fun for everyone, and owning it when it isn’t.
Closest friends
Negative Natalie, who balances his wild ideas, and Chill Panda, who believed him when no one else did.
Sparks fly with
Smarty Pants, who keeps finding his frogs.

Nobody in the Mighty Zoo is all good or all bad. A bad day is part of the story, never the whole story.

You know a Chaos Cat if…

  • Every plan becomes an adventure, whether you wanted one or not.
  • They're the reason everyone is laughing, and possibly the reason something is broken.
  • They dare you to try the thing you were scared to try.

How to love him

  • Make room for play on serious days.
  • Tell them kindly when a joke has gone too far.
  • Give their energy somewhere to go.

Stories and life lessons with Chaos Cat

Chaos Cat is the main character in 1 story and shows up in 2 more.

Home and family

Fearless Cub and the Other Side of the Fence

Fearless Cub slips out of the zoo to see the big world, and learns what home really is when the storm rolls in.

The lessonBe curious, and go explore. But the grass isn’t always greener on the other side. Home isn’t only where your heart is; it’s your shelter from the storm. When the sky turns dark, bet on family.

3 min read
Second chances

The Day Chaos Cat Got Blamed

When Panda’s lotus pots are knocked over, everyone is sure the mischief-maker did it. Everyone except Panda.

The lessonWhat someone did yesterday doesn’t decide what they did today. Check before you blame, and leave the door open for people to surprise you.

2 min read
Filling your own cup

Generous Piggy Says “Not Today”

Piggy gives away every snack, blanket and hug, until there’s nothing left for her. Then she snaps, and everyone is surprised.

The lessonYou can’t pour from an empty cup. Saying “not today” sometimes is how you keep having love to give.
