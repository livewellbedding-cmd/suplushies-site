Generous Piggy

“Here, take mine.”

Piggy's love language is sharing: snacks, blankets, the last hug of the night. The Mighty Zoo is teaching her that her own cup matters too.

Superpower
Generosity and warmth
Growing edge
Filling her own cup, too
Best match
Fearless Cub
Series
Series 1: The Mighty Zoo

Personality profile

The giver with the biggest heart in the zoo

On a good day
Remembers every birthday, shares every snack and turns up with a blanket before you knew you were cold.
On a bad day
Gives until there’s nothing left, then gets hungry, tired and snappy, and surprises everyone, herself included.
What’s really going on underneath
Being needed makes her feel loved. She worries that saying no means people will like her less.
Favorite spot in the Mighty Zoo
The Snack Stand, where she always seems to be handing something to someone.
Never without
A clear tummy pouch full of colorful treats, ready to share.
Says things like
“Here, take mine.”
“Did you eat? You didn’t eat.”
Learning to
Filling her own cup, too, and saying “not today” without guilt.
Closest friends
Fearless Cub, who protects her the way she looks after everyone else, and Workhorse.
Sparks fly with
Chaos Cat, who always wants just one more berry.

Nobody in the Mighty Zoo is all good or all bad. A bad day is part of the story, never the whole story.

You know a Generous Piggy if…

  • They give you the last snack, every time.
  • They remember everyone's birthday, and nobody remembers theirs.
  • They say yes before you finish asking.

How to love her

  • Give back before they run empty.
  • Make “no” feel safe to say.
  • Celebrate them on an ordinary Tuesday.

Stories and life lessons with Generous Piggy

Generous Piggy is the main character in 1 story and shows up in 2 more.

Moods aren’t labels

Natalie and the Cloudy Picnic

Natalie’s worrying spoils the fun, until the rain arrives and her worrying saves the day. Then two friends say sorry.

The lessonA gloomy mood is like weather. It rolls in, and it rolls out. Don’t let one cloudy moment become someone’s name, and that includes your own.

2 min read
Asking for help

Workhorse’s Grumpy Morning

Workhorse is up before the sun and too tired to be kind. A bad morning, it turns out, is not the same as a bad heart.

The lessonA bad morning doesn’t make a bad heart. When you snap, say sorry. Then rest, eat something, and let someone carry a bucket.

2 min read
Filling your own cup

Generous Piggy Says “Not Today”

Piggy gives away every snack, blanket and hug, until there’s nothing left for her. Then she snaps, and everyone is surprised.

The lessonYou can’t pour from an empty cup. Saying “not today” sometimes is how you keep having love to give.
