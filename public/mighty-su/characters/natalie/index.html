Negative Natalie

“If it can go wrong, it will.”

Natalie sees every cloud, and every silver lining she pretends not to notice. Underneath the sighs is a soft little bunny who just wants everything (and everyone) to be okay.

Superpower
Noticing what others miss
Growing edge
Leaving room for the rainbow
Best match
Chaos Cat
Series
Series 1: The Mighty Zoo

Personality profile

The worrier who notices what everyone else misses

On a good day
Spots the problem before it happens, packs the tarp nobody else thought of, and quietly saves the day.
On a bad day
Lets one worry turn into ten, and her gloomy forecasts dampen the fun before anything has gone wrong.
What’s really going on underneath
Her worrying is a way of caring. She wants everyone she loves to be okay, and she feels responsible when they aren’t.
Favorite spot in the Mighty Zoo
Under the big oak by the Big Lawn, where she can see the sky coming.
Never without
Her navy umbrella, carried rain or shine, and a red bow she straightens when she’s nervous.
Says things like
“Those clouds look like rain.”
“I’m just saying, someone should have a plan B.”
Learning to
Leaving room for the rainbow, and not letting one cloudy mood become her name.
Closest friends
Generous Piggy, who listens to every worry, and Smarty Pants, who now asks what she noticed.
Sparks fly with
Drama Llama, whose sunshine and Natalie’s clouds collide. Then they make up.

Nobody in the Mighty Zoo is all good or all bad. A bad day is part of the story, never the whole story.

You know a Negative Natalie if…

  • They pack an umbrella on a sunny day, just in case.
  • They see the problem nobody else has spotted yet.
  • Their worry is really a way of caring.

How to love her

  • Listen to the worry before you try to fix it.
  • Point out the small good things, gently.
  • Stand under the umbrella with them.

Stories and life lessons with Negative Natalie

Moods aren’t labels

Natalie and the Cloudy Picnic

Natalie’s worrying spoils the fun, until the rain arrives and her worrying saves the day. Then two friends say sorry.

The lessonA gloomy mood is like weather. It rolls in, and it rolls out. Don’t let one cloudy moment become someone’s name, and that includes your own.

2 min read
Filling your own cup

Generous Piggy Says “Not Today”

Piggy gives away every snack, blanket and hug, until there’s nothing left for her. Then she snaps, and everyone is surprised.

The lessonYou can’t pour from an empty cup. Saying “not today” sometimes is how you keep having love to give.

2 min read
It’s okay not to know

Smarty Pants and the Hedge Maze

Smarty Pants knows the way through the zoo’s new hedge maze. Definitely. Probably. Until three friends are very, very lost.

The lesson“I don’t know” is a brave sentence. The smartest thing you can do is ask, and listen to what your friends already know.
