Mighty Su · Series 1: The Mighty Zoo

Different lands. One home.

Mighty Su is an original SuPlushies character universe about belonging. In Series 1, eight animals from all over the world share one home, the Mighty Zoo, watched over by the Mighty Zookeeper. Nobody in the zoo is all good or all bad. They have good days and grumpy ones, just like us, and they keep finding their way back to each other.

The sage of the zoo

The Mighty Zookeeper

“Even the wildest hearts can find their rhythm.”

The Zookeeper watches over the Mighty Zoo but almost never steps in. The animals make their own mistakes and find their own way back. Sometimes, at the very end of a story, you'll catch the Zookeeper smiling from the gate.

The Zookeeper is drawn from Su Chen, the founder of SuPlushies, and carries Su's belief that a family is something you build together.

One cast, many worlds

How the Mighty Su series works

Every Mighty Su series keeps the same characters and moves them to a new setting, and Su takes on a new role in each one.

  1. Series 1 · Now

    The Mighty Zoo

    Su is the Mighty Zookeeper. Every story takes place in the zoo: the gate, the Big Lawn, the feed barn, the bamboo grove, the pond and the Talent Night stage.

  2. Series 2 · Coming later

    The Mighty School

    Same friends, new hallways. Su becomes the Mighty Principal.

  3. Future series

    More worlds to come

    New places for the same family to grow up, mess up and make up.

Why Mighty Su

A story about belonging, from someone who went looking for it

Su Chen came to the United States from China and spent years doing what so many immigrants do: working hard, learning the rules, and looking for a place to belong.

Somewhere along the way, Su noticed that belonging rarely comes from sharing a background. It comes from people who take the time to understand your lived experience, and let you understand theirs. That's what a family is, whoever is in it.

Mighty Su is that idea, told through a zoo. Every animal comes from a different land. Every one of them has a bad day now and then. And every one of them has a home.
